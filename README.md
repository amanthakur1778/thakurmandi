## 🚀 Key Features of Chalo Himachal

1. **Universal Travel Search Engine**:
   - Destination autocomplete for Himachal regions (Manali & Rohtang, Shimla & Kufri, Dharamshala & McLeodGanj, Spiti Valley & Kaza, Kasol & Parvati, Jibhi & Tirthan, Dalhousie & Khajjiar, Kinnaur & Sangla, Bir Billing).
   - Check-in & Check-out date pickers.
   - Interactive Guests & Rooms modal counter (Adults, Children, Rooms).
   - Instant search filtering with live feedback toasts.

2. **Signature TripAdvisor Rating System**:
   - **5-Bubble Rating Meter**: Classic green traveler bubbles (`●●●●●`) with review counts.
   - **Travellers' Choice 2026 Badges**: Featured on top Himachal stays and mountain treks.
   - **Community Reviews Feed**: Verified traveler ratings and trip reports from Himachal holidays.
   - **Interactive "Write a Chalo Himachal Review" Modal**: Users can rate (1 to 5 bubbles), pick trip type (Family, Solo, Couples, Friends, Business), and publish reviews dynamically.

3. **Himachal Tourism 8-Destination Explorer (Devbhoomi Experience)**:
   - **Manali, Solang Valley & Rohtang Pass**: Snow peaks, Atal Tunnel, Beas river rafting, Old Manali cafes.
   - **Shimla, Mall Road & Kufri Trails**: Colonial heritage, Christ Church, UNESCO toy train, Jakhoo ropeway.
   - **Dharamshala, McLeodGanj & Triund**: Dalai Lama Temple, Tibetan culture, Moon Peak snowline ridge.
   - **Spiti Valley, Key Gompa & Chandra Taal**: High-altitude cold desert, 1000-year monasteries, Moon Lake.
   - **Jibhi & Tirthan Valley**: Authentic mudhouses, trout streams, Great Himalayan National Park (UNESCO).
   - **Kasol & Parvati Valley**: Riverside deodar woods, German bakeries, Kheerganga hot springs.
   - **Dalhousie & Khajjiar**: Mini Switzerland of India, Kalatop forest sanctuary, Dainkund peak.
   - **Kinnaur & Sangla Valley**: Chitkul (Last Indian Village), Baspa riverbanks, sacred Kinner Kailash.

4. **100% Himachal Stays, Treks & Mountain Cafes**:
   - **Himachal Stays** (5-Star Luxury, Authentic Homestays, Mountain Chalets, Glamping & High Camps):
     - *Luxury*: Wildflower Hall Shimla, The Oberoi Cecil Shimla, Span Resort & Spa Manali, Hyatt Regency Dharamshala, Fortune Park Moksha, Grand View Dalhousie.
     - *Homestays*: The Mudhouse Jibhi, Traditional Kathkuni Apple Orchard Naggar, Spiti Heritage Mud Homestay Langza, Chitkul Baspa Riverside Homestay, Riverside Deodar Kasol, Barot Valley Trout Homestay.

   - **Himachal Treks & Adventures** (High Alpine Treks, Snow Safaris, Aerial Sports, UNESCO Heritage):
