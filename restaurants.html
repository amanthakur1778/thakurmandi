Authentic Pahadi Flavors

What is a Himachali Dham?

Dham is a traditional multi-course vegetarian banquet prepared by specialized hereditary cooks called Botis. Served on biodegradable leaf plates (Pattals), it features Madra (chickpeas in slow-cooked curd and whole spices), Sepu Vadi, Mah ki Dal, and sweet rice (Meethe Chawal). No trip to Himachal is complete without it!
