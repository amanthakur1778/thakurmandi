The Kathkuni Tradition

What Makes Himachali Homestays Unique?

Kathkuni is an indigenous, earthquake-resistant architectural marvel made by alternating dry stone and deodar wood without any cement or nails. Paired with clay plaster and slate roofs, these homes stay cool in summer and cozy during sub-zero winters. Aman Thakur connects you directly with the host families.
