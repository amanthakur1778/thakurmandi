Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries answered by Aman Thakur regarding Himachal travel, seasons, and roads.

The peak snowfall season in Himachal Pradesh is from late December through early March. Manali, Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Kufri (Shimla), and Sissu (Lahaul) receive heavy snowfall during these months. For winter snow, Aman arranges 4x4 snow chain vehicles and warm insulated chalets.
Yes, Rohtang Pass requires an official National Green Tribunal (NGT) vehicle permit which has daily quotas. Spiti Valley requires Inner Line Permits (ILP) for international tourists visiting regions near the Tibetan border like Chitkul, Khab, and Nako. Aman Thakur arranges all mandatory permits in advance.
